- **U.S. stocks started May on a strong note**: The **S&P 500 hit a record close**, helped by **cooling oil prices** and a **lift from Apple**, signaling investors are leaning risk-on despite lingering macro uncertainty.

- **Apple reassured markets**: A **strong revenue outlook** suggests the company is navigating **supply shortages and rising chip costs** better than feared, reinforcing mega-cap tech’s role as the market’s stabilizer.

- **Oil majors beat, but energy stocks slipped**: **Exxon and Chevron** posted resilient earnings thanks to production strength, yet **falling oil prices** weighed on the sector—good for inflation sentiment, less so for oil equities.

- **Japan’s currency intervention is back in focus**: After a reported **$34.5 billion yen intervention**, traders are bracing for more action, highlighting how fragile FX markets remain as policymakers resist excessive currency weakness.

- **Defense AI is becoming a major commercial lane**: The **Pentagon struck deals with top AI firms** including Microsoft, Amazon, and Google to bring AI into classified networks—another sign that government/security spending is becoming a key monetization path for big AI.

- **AI/software sentiment improved on earnings**: **Atlassian, Twilio, and Five9** rallied after earnings beats tied to AI initiatives, pushing back on the “**SaaSpocalypse**” narrative that AI would simply commoditize software.

- **OpenAI faces a reality check**: Even as its CFO says demand could go “vertical,” reports that the company **missed internal growth targets** suggest AI enthusiasm is still running ahead of execution in some parts of the market.

- **Robotaxis are moving from hype to rollout—and regulation**: Expansion across U.S. cities is accelerating, but **California’s new enforcement powers over driverless cars** show commercialization will come with tighter oversight.

- **Consumer-health trends are creating odd winners**: Side effects linked to **GLP-1 weight-loss drugs** are reportedly boosting **gum demand**, a small but telling example of how healthcare shifts can ripple into unexpected consumer categories.

- **Travel stress point to watch**: **Spirit Airlines** may receive an “America First” rescue, underscoring how **fuel-market disruption** and geopolitical shocks are pressuring weaker carriers first.
