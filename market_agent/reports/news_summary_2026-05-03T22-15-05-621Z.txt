  - The U.S. military reached agreements with **seven tech firms** to use AI on **classified systems**.

- **California to ticket autonomous vehicles via manufacturers**
  - California will begin issuing traffic-law citations for driverless vehicles directly to the **manufacturer** when AVs violate rules.
  - This is a regulatory development relevant to autonomous-vehicle operators such as **Waymo and others**.

- **Elon Musk/OpenAI trial underway**
  - A federal judge reportedly rebuked Elon Musk as trial proceedings opened in Musk’s case against **OpenAI** and Sam Altman.

- **Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang pushes back on AI job-loss narrative**
  - Huang said some CEOs have a **“God complex”** in framing AI apocalypse scenarios and warned that such messaging could worsen shortages of critical workers.
  - Relevant for AI labor-market sentiment and enterprise adoption narratives.

- **Gas prices rising nationwide**
  - ABC reported U.S. gas prices are moving higher, with regional differences in the most and least expensive states.
  - Higher fuel prices can affect consumer spending, transportation, and inflation expectations.
