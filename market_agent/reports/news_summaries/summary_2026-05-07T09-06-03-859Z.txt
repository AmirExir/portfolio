- **Markets stay in rally mode:** U.S. futures moved higher after fresh record closes, with tech earnings and AI spending continuing to power optimism across the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and global equities.

- **AI remains the market’s main engine:** Strong results from **AMD** and upbeat guidance from **Super Micro** reinforced the data-center buildout theme, suggesting AI infrastructure demand is still broadening beyond the biggest megacaps.

- **Novo Nordisk got a major boost from obesity drugs:** Sales of its newly launched **Wegovy pill** beat expectations, prompting the company to raise guidance — another sign the GLP-1 market is expanding fast and supporting healthcare growth leaders.

- **Disney is in focus as new leadership faces its first big test:** Investors are watching the company’s earnings closely for signs of strategic direction and execution under recently installed CEO **Josh D’Amaro**.

- **Oil prices eased on hopes of lower geopolitical disruption:** Prices slipped as the U.S. pushed for reopening trade through the **Strait of Hormuz**, though markets remain cautious until there’s evidence of real progress.

- **Regulation is tightening around AI:** The U.S. government will begin testing major AI models from **Google, Microsoft, and xAI** before release, signaling more formal oversight of frontier AI systems.

- **Big Tech’s AI legal and reputational risks are rising:** **Apple** agreed to a $250 million settlement over alleged AI marketing claims, while **Meta** was sued by publishers over claims it used copyrighted books to train AI models.

- **Healthcare insurers are responding to pressure:** **UnitedHealthcare** said it will remove prior authorization requirements for about **30% of services**, a notable operational shift amid scrutiny over delayed and denied care.

- **Airlines and consumer companies show mixed cost signals:** **Delta** is cutting free snacks and drinks on many short-haul flights, while **Chipotle** is again defending portion-size complaints — reminders that customer experience remains a pressure point even as companies manage margins.

- **FDA policy shift could reshape vaping competition:** The agency approved **fruit-flavored vape products** for the first time, a potentially meaningful move for tobacco and vaping players as the regulatory landscape evolves.
