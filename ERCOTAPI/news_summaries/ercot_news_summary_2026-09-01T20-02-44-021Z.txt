- ERCOT posted a cluster of market-rule updates that tighten how scarcity-era resources and fuel costs are reflected in market outcomes: NPRR1347 would set Energy Offer Curves for Contract for Capacity resources at effective VOLL, while NPRR1345 extends the Exceptional Fuel Cost process to 2030 and NPRR1346 increases the fuel-oil transportation adder. Net effect: stronger administrative support for dispatchable resource cost recovery and scarcity performance during reliability events. (ERCOT NPRR1347, NPRR1345, NPRR1346)
- ERCOT advanced a congestion/market-efficiency change via NPRR1343, which would charge a bid fee for inefficient hourly PTP Obligation bids in DAM. This is aimed at reducing low-value virtual congestion activity and improving day-ahead market efficiency, with potential impacts on CRR/PTP trading behavior and DAM liquidity. (ERCOT NPRR1343)
- ERCOT is moving governance documents deeper into formal rule structure: NPRR1344 would place the Texas Market Test Plan into Protocol Section 19, standardizing approvals rather than leaving it as a separate OBD. A new TAC ballot file for this item was also posted, indicating the proposal is progressing through stakeholder governance even though the spreadsheet itself was not readable. (ERCOT NPRR1344; 1344NPRR-07 TAC Ballot 082626)
- ERCOT resurfaced NPRR917 on nodal pricing for Settlement Only Distribution Generators and Settlement Only Transmission Generators. If implemented, this would shift these settlement-only resources from zonal to nodal energy pricing, improving locational price accuracy and aligning settlement incentives more closely with actual grid conditions. (ERCOT NPRR917)
- Large load interconnection and modeling remain a major ERCOT planning theme: PGRR145 creates a “Batch Zero” process to study mature large-load interconnections on a system-wide basis, and PGRR144 tightens dynamic model submission requirements for large loads including large computational loads. Together, these changes point to more rigorous screening of data centers and other large demand before interconnection decisions and network upgrades. (ERCOT PGRR145, PGRR144)
- A newly posted TAC report shows PGRR144 was recommended for approval on Aug. 26, 2026. That strengthens ERCOT’s ability to require better dynamic load models for large loads/LCLs, which is critical for transient stability, fault response, and planning-case accuracy as load growth concentrates in specific zones. (ERCOT 144PGRR-19 TAC Report 082626; PGRR144)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR289 to allow certain Large Computational Loads up to 365 days after in-service to comply with cooling-load ride-through requirements. This may ease near-term interconnection timing for some LCL projects, but it also highlights ERCOT’s effort to balance rapid large-load energization against the need for verified disturbance ride-through performance. (ERCOT NOGRR289)
- ERCOT continues adapting reliability products for storage: NOGRR288, tied to NPRR1340, adds Energy Storage Resource participation to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service. This expands the pool of fast-response reliability capacity and should improve reserve flexibility, but it also increases the importance of operating-guide clarity around state of charge, deployment, and sustained performance. (ERCOT NOGRR288)
- Transmission and operations planning updates continue to target higher-voltage and emergency performance: NOGRR286 adds 765-kV operational voltage limits, while PGRR147 aligns allowable non-consequential load loss treatment with NERC for Category P6 contingencies. These changes support ERCOT’s move toward larger backbone facilities and clearer reliability criteria for extreme but credible contingencies. (ERCOT NOGRR286, PGRR147)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR287 to improve load-shed coordination so TOs and TDSPs better identify and minimize overlap of essential loads during emergency interruptions. Operationally, this should help preserve critical service during firm load shed events and sharpen outage-block design after recent reliability stress events. (ERCOT NOGRR287)
- ERCOT also updated OBDRR055 covering Non-Spin deployment and recall procedures for RTC+B alignment. While procedural, this matters operationally because reserve deployment logic affects how quickly ERCOT can convert procured ancillary capacity into real balancing support as system conditions tighten. (ERCOT OBDRR055)
