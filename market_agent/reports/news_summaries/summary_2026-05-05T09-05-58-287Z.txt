- **Energy shock is driving the macro mood:** U.S. stock futures edged higher, but markets remain fixated on the Iran conflict, the Strait of Hormuz disruption, and a **30+ cent jump in U.S. gas prices** in a week. OPEC+ offered only a **modest production increase**, which may do little to offset near-term supply anxiety.

- **Inflation fears are back on the radar:** Minneapolis Fed President **Neel Kashkari** warned the Iran war could keep inflation elevated and make **rate cuts less certain**. Translation: geopolitics is becoming a direct monetary-policy risk again.

- **Samsung’s ruling family closes the book on a historic tax bill:** The family of late chairman **Lee Kun-hee** has now paid off an **$8 billion inheritance tax**, underscoring both the scale of Samsung’s wealth and the financial burden of succession at major family-controlled conglomerates.

- **GameStop makes a highly audacious M&A play:** The meme-stock retailer is reportedly pursuing a **$56 billion cash-and-stock takeover of eBay**. If serious, it’s a striking attempt by Ryan Cohen to transform GameStop from a legacy retailer into a far larger e-commerce platform.

- **AI capital markets momentum continues:** **Anthropic** is nearing a **$1.5 billion joint venture with Wall Street firms**, another sign that leading AI companies are moving beyond model development into deeper financial-sector commercialization.

- **Airline sector turmoil is widening:** After **Spirit Airlines’ abrupt collapse**, a crowdsourced online campaign quickly drew attention with millions in informal pledges. Separately, **Delta canceled hundreds of flights**, with reliability issues reportedly at risk of lasting into summer—highlighting fragility in budget and legacy airline operations alike.

- **Berkshire’s post-Buffett era looks steady so far:** **Greg Abel** received broadly positive reviews from shareholders after his first annual meeting at the helm, suggesting investors see continuity—not disruption—as the key value proposition.

- **AI in healthcare may be nearing a credibility milestone:** A Harvard-linked study found AI systems delivered **more accurate emergency-room diagnoses than two human doctors** in tested scenarios, reinforcing the commercial and clinical significance of medical AI.

- **Consumer and retail signals are mixed:** McDonald’s is reportedly **removing self-serve soda stations nationwide**, reflecting broader operational changes in fast food, while Costco’s return of a popular meal kit points to continued demand for **value-oriented convenience spending**.

- **Food safety and consumer trust matter again in Europe:** Austrian police arrested a suspect after **rat poison was found in HiPP baby food jars**, a reminder that supply-chain trust and brand risk remain material business issues, especially in packaged food.
