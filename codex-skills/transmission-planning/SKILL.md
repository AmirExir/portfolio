# Transmission Planning

## Use this skill when

Use this skill for steady-state planning, N-0/N-1 analysis, generator or large-load interconnection, transfer capability, topology review, violation screening, mitigation development, or study-report preparation.

## Required workflow

1. Define the study question and decision being supported.
2. Identify base case, year, season, load level, dispatch, topology, and applicable criteria.
3. Identify monitored elements and contingency set.
4. Validate base-case convergence and reasonableness.
5. Run the requested scenarios consistently.
6. Separate new impacts from pre-existing conditions.
7. Quantify severity, sensitivity, and repeatability.
8. Develop and compare credible mitigations.
9. Document assumptions, exclusions, and uncertainty.

## Analysis rules

- Distinguish thermal, voltage, stability, short-circuit, and operational issues.
- Distinguish pre-existing violations from project-caused impacts.
- Distinguish normal, emergency, and load-shed conditions.
- Check whether a mitigation creates new violations elsewhere.
- Avoid recommending facilities solely from one solved snapshot.
- Consider dispatch, outage, and topology sensitivities.
- Explain the physical mechanism behind major impacts.

## Interconnection screening

For generation or storage, consider:

- POI strength and topology
- deliverability and congestion
- local voltage support
- reactive capability
- outage sensitivity
- stability and system-strength concerns
- curtailment exposure

For large loads and data centers, consider:

- ramp and energization behavior
- coincident peak assumptions
- redundancy and service configuration
- voltage and reactive demand
- contingency exposure
- phased buildout
- behind-the-meter generation or storage

## Mitigation assessment

Compare options using:

- technical effectiveness
- scope and constructability
- outage requirements
- cost-order magnitude
- schedule
- operational complexity
- sensitivity to future assumptions

## Reporting standard

Include:

- Study objective
- Cases and scenarios
- Criteria
- Key assumptions
- Results
- Engineering interpretation
- Mitigation options
- Risks and limitations
