- **Fed turns more political, but rate cuts still look distant:** The Senate confirmed **Kevin Warsh** as Fed chair in a near party-line vote, underscoring how politicized the central bank has become. Even so, the bigger market message is **higher-for-longer rates**, not imminent easing.

- **Markets hit fresh highs on AI/tech leadership:** The **S&P 500 and Nasdaq reached new records**, driven mainly by big tech and chip names. It’s a bullish tape, but notably **narrow leadership**—the rally depends heavily on AI winners.

- **Bond market flashes inflation warning:** Investors got **5% on 30-year Treasuries for the first time since 2007**, while Boston Fed President Collins even flagged a possible **rate-hike scenario**. Translation: the bond market is saying inflation risk is still very real.

- **Oil holds firm as geopolitics dominate:** Crude steadied ahead of **Trump-Xi talks**, with the **Iran conflict** and risks around the **Strait of Hormuz** keeping energy markets tense. That raises the odds of **renewed inflation pressure** if supply fears worsen.

- **AI boom keeps capital flowing:** **Cerebras raised its IPO price** to target **$5.5 billion**, a sign that investor appetite for AI remains strong. Nvidia also rallied as optimism around AI demand and U.S.-China ties lifted chip stocks.

- **Tech is still hiring for AI by firing elsewhere:** **Cisco posted record revenue** but announced **about 4,000 job cuts**, redirecting spending toward AI, security, and optics. **LinkedIn** is also laying off staff while changing operations—classic efficiency-plus-AI reallocation.

- **Airline consolidation continues in a tough market:** **Allegiant completed its acquisition of Sun Country**, creating a larger low-cost carrier. The deal suggests budget airlines see **scale as essential** amid industry turbulence.

- **Crypto legislation gains momentum:** Sen. **John Kennedy’s support** boosts prospects for a Senate crypto bill, potentially marking a **meaningful step toward clearer U.S. regulation** for digital assets.

- **Musk empire remains in the spotlight:** The **Musk v. Altman/OpenAI trial** is wrapping up, while **xAI** faces scrutiny over unregulated gas turbines at a Mississippi data center—highlighting both the **legal drama** and **infrastructure strain** behind the AI race.

- **Regional disruption risk:** In New York, a potential **Long Island Rail Road strike** could force many commuters to work from home, a reminder that **labor disruptions still carry local economic impact**.
