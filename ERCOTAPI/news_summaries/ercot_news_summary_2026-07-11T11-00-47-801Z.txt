Planning implication: ERCOT and TSPs likely face more scrutiny on load forecasting, interconnection studies, curtailment frameworks, and whether flexible load can be integrated without degrading reliability. (Associated Press; PRNewswire; The Real Deal)

(CleanTechnica)
