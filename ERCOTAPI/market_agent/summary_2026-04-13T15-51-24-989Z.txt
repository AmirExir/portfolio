- Texas is providing data centers more than $1 billion a year in sales-tax breaks, according to Texas Tribune reporting highlighted by Grist and TechRadar. For ERCOT, that matters because state incentives are still pulling in very large flexible and inflexible loads even as policymakers question whether the fiscal support is sustainable. (Texas Tribune/Grist/TechRadar)

- Multiple reports point to accelerating Texas data center buildout, including Aligned Data Centers’ new 540 MW “Project Caprock” campus in Northwest Texas and broader investor focus on Abilene, Permian, and other Texas AI infrastructure hubs. That scale implies major new ERCOT load growth, with likely pressure on transmission upgrades, interconnection studies, local congestion management, and resource adequacy planning if these projects move from announcement to energized load. (GlobeNewswire/Business Insider/IBTimes.com.au)

- The same AI-led growth story is also reinforcing expectations for more gas-fired infrastructure and pipeline demand, while several national reports note project delays from supply-chain bottlenecks, power costs, and community opposition. For ERCOT, that combination suggests a split outlook: near-term load ramp timing may be uneven, but the long-run planning challenge remains substantial because generation, gas deliverability, and grid expansion may all need to scale to serve large-load interconnections reliably. (Fortune/OilPrice.com/TechRadar/Harvard Gazette)
