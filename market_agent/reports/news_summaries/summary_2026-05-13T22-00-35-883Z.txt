- **Retail restructuring deepens:** Walmart is cutting or relocating about **1,000 corporate roles** as it merges tech and product teams, underscoring how even defensive giants are still chasing efficiency through organizational consolidation.

- **Inflation reaccelerates on energy shock:** U.S. CPI reportedly rose **3.8% year over year**, with the Iran war lifting gasoline prices. The key takeaway: inflation is proving geopolitically sensitive again, complicating any near-term rate-cut hopes.

- **Oil risk is now a broader market issue:** Separate reports warn that disrupted shipping and falling crude stockpiles could turn the current oil squeeze into a more severe supply problem. If sustained, this would pressure margins, consumers, and central banks simultaneously.

- **Tariff refunds start flowing:** Companies are beginning to receive refunds after the Supreme Court struck down some Trump-era tariffs. That’s a quiet but meaningful boost to cash flow for affected importers and manufacturers.

- **Fed politics remain tricky:** Kevin Warsh’s arrival at the Fed is drawing attention, but hotter inflation and internal policy divisions may limit how quickly he can align with Trump’s preference for lower rates.

- **Big Tech/AI scrutiny intensifies:** Sam Altman testified about a “hair-raising” past safety discussion with Elon Musk, while separate reports say his business dealings are facing GOP scrutiny ahead of a possible OpenAI IPO. AI is increasingly a governance and regulatory story, not just a growth story.

- **AI liability risks are rising:** A lawsuit alleging ChatGPT encouraged a deadly drug mix highlights a growing business risk for AI firms: product liability, trust, and safety could become major cost centers.

- **GM joins layoff wave:** Newly laid-off GM employees described abrupt terminations, with AI reportedly part of the backdrop. The broader signal is that automation pressure is moving from theory to workforce reality across corporate America.

- **Amazon pushes the convenience frontier again:** The company is expanding **30-minute delivery hubs**, aiming to redefine “fast” retail one more time. That could raise consumer expectations industrywide, but it also puts fresh pressure on logistics economics.

- **Labor costs are climbing in travel:** United flight attendants ratified a new deal including **31% raises**, showing how labor is still winning larger wage gains in service sectors where staffing is critical.

- **Nvidia’s China ambitions face political friction:** Jensen Huang wants chip sales in China, but U.S. policy priorities may not cooperate. For investors, the issue remains whether AI demand can stay global while export controls grow tighter.

- **Space-based computing moves from sci-fi to strategy:** Google and SpaceX are reportedly discussing orbital data centers. It’s speculative for now, but it shows how seriously hyperscalers are thinking about long-term AI infrastructure constraints.
