- ERCOT posted a cluster of new market-rule items centered on fuel-cost recovery and reliability procurement: NPRR1346 would double the transportation adder in the Fuel Oil Price from $0.05/gal to $0.10/gal, while NPRR1345 would extend the Exceptional Fuel Cost process sunset from 2027 to 2030—signaling continued emphasis on compensating costly emergency fuel arrangements and preserving thermal unit availability during stressed conditions. (ERCOT NPRR1346; NPRR1345)

- ERCOT is refining market incentives and mitigation rules: NPRR1343 proposes a Day-Ahead Market fee on inefficient hourly PTP Obligation bids, which could discourage uneconomic congestion trading behavior and improve DAM efficiency, while NPRR1342 would create a specific Mitigated Offer Cap process for Contract for Capacity resources tied to ERCOT emergency procurement. (ERCOT NPRR1343; NPRR1342)

- Governance and rule-control changes continue: NPRR1344 would move the Texas Market Test Plan from an Other Binding Document into Protocol Section 19, tightening formal approval and change-control over testing processes that affect market implementation and system changes. (ERCOT NPRR1344)

- ERCOT posted a notable large-load reliability update: NOGRR289 would give Large Computational Loads up to 365 days after in-service to comply with cooling-load ride-through requirements, easing initial interconnection timing but potentially extending the period where fast-changing large loads are not yet fully aligned with disturbance-performance expectations. (ERCOT NOGRR289)

- ERCOT is continuing to adapt operating rules for large flexible loads and storage: NOGRR288 would add Energy Storage Resource participation to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service, expanding the pool of fast-response reliability assets, while NOGRR287 would improve load-shed coordination to reduce overlap with essential loads during emergency curtailments. (ERCOT NOGRR288; NOGRR287)

- Transmission and operations standards are moving higher-voltage and contingency planning forward: NOGRR286 would add 765-kV operational voltage limits, an important step for future extra-high-voltage backbone operation, and PGRR147 aligns allowable non-consequential load loss treatment with NERC for P6/N-1-1 events, affecting how planners evaluate severe contingencies and compliance risk. (ERCOT NOGRR286; PGRR147)

- ERCOT continues tightening planning requirements around large loads and interconnection transparency: PGRR144 clarifies dynamic model submission and review requirements for Large Loads including large computational loads, improving stability-study fidelity, and PGRR131 mandates more formal interconnection cost reporting for transmission-connected generators, increasing transparency for project developers and planners. (ERCOT PGRR144; PGRR131)

- Interconnection and study process rules are being formalized further: PGRR132 clarifies that new Resources must interconnect through a new Standard Generation Interconnection Agreement, while PGRR146 changes the stability assessment cadence for planned large generators and Large Loads from quarterly to every four months, indicating ERCOT is recalibrating study workload as the queue mix evolves. (ERCOT PGRR132; PGRR146)

- ERCOT also posted OBDRR055 updating Non-Spinning Reserve deployment and recall procedures for RTC+B alignment; even without a full procedural readout here, the title indicates operating instructions are being synchronized with newer real-time commitment/dispatch logic, which matters for reserve performance and operator actions during tight conditions. (ERCOT OBDRR055)
