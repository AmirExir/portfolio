- Microsoft has entered exclusive negotiations with Chevron and Engine No. 1 to develop a $7 billion gas power plant in West Texas, which aims to supply electricity to a large data center campus. This project could significantly increase demand on ERCOT's grid and intensify discussions about grid reliability and capacity. (Financial Post)

- The potential addition of this power plant comes amid rising concerns about grid performance and the need for robust infrastructure to support increasing demand from technology and data-driven industries in Texas. The intersection of energy and digital infrastructure will likely shape future resource planning in ERCOT. (OilPrice.com)
