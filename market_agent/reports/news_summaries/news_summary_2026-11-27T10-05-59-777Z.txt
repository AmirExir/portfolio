Source: CNA, Nov 26, 2026, 3:30 AM CST, https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/oil-prices-fall-supply-concerns-linger-after-iran-claims-mine-strikes-ships-6583906

What to watch:
- NVDA — watch for any direct Nvidia statement on Southeast Asia shipment restrictions; confirmed halts would be bearish for regional demand visibility, while clarification that limits are narrow or temporary would soften that risk.
- Norse Atlantic — watch for emergency financing or a bankruptcy filing; fresh funding would be less bearish than a formal insolvency process.
- CLSK / Bitcoin miners — watch whether treasury sales exceed mined output in coming quarters; that would be bearish for balance-sheet stress signals unless losses narrow materially.
- Oil — watch for verified damage, shipping interruptions, or official military/port statements tied to Hormuz and Saudi refining assets; confirmed disruption would be bullish for crude, while de-escalation would ease supply-premium risk.
