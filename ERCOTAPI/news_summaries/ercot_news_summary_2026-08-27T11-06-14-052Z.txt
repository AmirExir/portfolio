- ERCOT posted a cluster of market-rule updates centered on fuel cost recovery for thermal reliability: NPRR1345 would extend the Exceptional Fuel Cost Process sunset from April 1, 2027 to April 1, 2030, while NPRR1346 would raise the Fuel Oil Price transportation adder from $0.05/gal to $0.10/gal—together signaling ERCOT still sees emergency fuel logistics and oil-backed generation costs as operationally relevant for reliability events. (ERCOT NPRR1345, NPRR1346)
- ERCOT posted new large-load reliability rule changes that tighten performance expectations for data-center-style demand: NOGRR282 would establish frequency and voltage ride-through requirements for Large Computational Loads, while NOGRR289 would allow certain LCL cooling load components up to 365 days after in-service to comply—showing ERCOT is moving from simply studying large loads to enforcing grid-support behavior with limited transition relief. (ERCOT NOGRR282, NOGRR289)
- ERCOT advanced planning-rule changes for large load interconnection oversight: PGRR145 would create a “Batch Zero” process to study mature large-load interconnection requests on a system-wide basis, and PGRR144 would clarify dynamic model submission requirements for Large Loads including LCLs. Grid impact: better upfront modeling of voltage/frequency behavior and a more centralized screen for coincident load additions that could strain local transmission and stability margins. (ERCOT PGRR145, PGRR144)
- ERCOT posted a market design change aimed at reducing inefficient DAM congestion trading: NPRR1343 proposes a bid fee for each inefficient hourly Point-to-Point Obligation bid in the Day-Ahead Market. If adopted, that could deter low-value virtual congestion positions, improve DAM efficiency, and reduce uplift or distortions tied to uneconomic transmission bidding behavior. (ERCOT NPRR1343)
- ERCOT posted a reliability procurement pricing change for Contract for Capacity resources: NPRR1342 would create a new process for setting Mitigated Offer Caps for C4C resources procured under ERCOT’s control area authority provisions. The likely market implication is tighter guardrails on emergency capacity compensation and clearer mitigation treatment when ERCOT procures out-of-market reliability support. (ERCOT NPRR1342)
- ERCOT posted a governance/process cleanup item: NPRR1344 would move the Texas Market Test Plan from an Other Binding Document into Protocol Section 19, standardizing approvals. This is not a direct operations change, but it matters for market implementation governance because testing requirements would sit more explicitly inside the formal Protocol structure. (ERCOT NPRR1344)
- ERCOT posted several operating-guide changes with direct reliability/planning implications beyond large loads: NOGRR286 adds 765-kV operational voltage limits to support reliable operation of new extra-high-voltage equipment, and NOGRR287 would refine load-shed provisions so TOs/TDSPs better identify and minimize overlap of essential loads during outages. Together these point to ERCOT preparing both for higher-voltage grid expansion and more precise emergency operations. (ERCOT NOGRR286, NOGRR287)
- ERCOT posted storage and ancillary-service updates: NOGRR288, related to NPRR1340, would add Energy Storage Resource participation to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service, while OBDRR055 updates Non-Spin deployment and recall procedures for RTC+B alignment. Operationally, this supports deeper battery integration into reliability products and cleaner real-time deployment processes. (ERCOT NOGRR288, OBDRR055)
- ERCOT also posted planning/compliance refinements that are less headline-driven but still meaningful: PGRR146 would shift large generator and large load stability assessments from every three months to every four months, PGRR147 aligns allowable non-consequential load loss treatment with NERC for P6 N-1-1 contingencies, and NOGRR279 extends high-resolution recording equipment deadlines to 2029. Net effect: ERCOT is tuning study cadence, contingency compliance, and telemetry rollout as system complexity grows. (ERCOT PGRR146, PGRR147, NOGRR279)
