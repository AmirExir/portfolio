- **Markets hit new highs:** U.S. stocks rallied broadly, with the **Dow up 790 points** and the **S&P 500/Nasdaq at record closes**, suggesting risk appetite remains strong despite macro and geopolitical noise.

- **Apple lifted sentiment, again:** **Apple beat earnings expectations** on strong iPhone and services demand, helping futures rise. Investors also focused on management’s **tariff-refund strategy**, which signals active policy navigation and potential margin support.

- **Not all EV beats are rewarded:** **Rivian topped Q1 expectations**, but the stock still fell about **5% after hours** as investors zeroed in on **continued losses and launch-related costs**—a reminder that execution and cash burn still matter more than headline beats.

- **AI is boosting growth—but also costs:** The broader U.S. economy reportedly got support from **AI-related investment**, even as **consumer spending softened**. Meanwhile, **Meta’s AI spending** was cited as a factor behind planned layoffs of **8,000 employees**, underscoring the uneven corporate impact of the AI race.

- **Labor market remains remarkably tight:** **Weekly jobless claims fell to 189,000**, the lowest in more than 50 years, reinforcing the view that the job market remains resilient even as growth becomes more uneven.

- **Japan stepped into FX markets:** The **yen surged 3%** after Japan reportedly intervened following a “final” warning on currency weakness, highlighting growing official discomfort with excessive volatility and the global sensitivity to policy moves.

- **Energy infrastructure got a political tailwind:** President Trump gave key approval to a **major Canada-U.S. oil pipeline expansion**, a sign of continued support for fossil-fuel infrastructure, though further regulatory approvals are still needed.

- **Media megamerger faces bigger pushback:** More Republican state attorneys general joined the lawsuit to block the **$6.2 billion Nexstar-Tegna merger**, increasing antitrust pressure on large-scale consolidation in local TV.

- **Reddit joined the earnings winners:** **Reddit shares jumped** after a Q1 beat, showing investor appetite remains strong for internet platforms that can deliver better-than-expected monetization and growth.

- **Policy and governance themes are intensifying:** Washington is pressing tech firms on **AI-driven cyber risks**, while senators from both parties are pushing tighter rules on **prediction markets**, signaling deeper regulatory scrutiny across emerging digital markets.
