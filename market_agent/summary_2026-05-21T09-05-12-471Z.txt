- **Markets pulled back as inflation and bond yields reasserted pressure.** U.S. stocks fell for a third straight session, and Asian markets were poised to follow, showing investors are again sensitive to sticky inflation and higher-for-longer rates.

- **Nvidia earnings are the market’s key near-term catalyst.** Futures were cautious ahead of results, underscoring how much broader sentiment still hinges on AI spending and whether Nvidia can keep justifying elevated valuations.

- **Meta began sweeping layoffs, cutting roughly 10% of staff.** The move signals that even mega-cap tech is still prioritizing efficiency and margin discipline despite the AI boom.

- **AI remains a paradox: commercially dominant, politically vulnerable.** New research from Nature highlights rapid progress in AI-assisted scientific software, while WSJ reports growing public backlash to AI expansion, including opposition to data centers and broader trust concerns.

- **Anthropic added another top AI name.** Andrej Karpathy joining the company reinforces how competition in generative AI is increasingly about talent concentration as much as model performance.

- **The SEC unveiled major IPO rule changes aimed at reviving listings.** The overhaul could make going public easier and comes as private-market giants continue delaying IPOs—though appetite will still depend on valuations and rate conditions.

- **SpaceX IPO speculation continues to energize private-market investors.** Reports suggest a future listing could generate record venture returns, highlighting how a few late-stage private companies still dominate exit expectations.

- **DOJ indicted major container manufacturers and executives over alleged global price-fixing.** The case could renew scrutiny on supply-chain concentration and shipping costs, with implications for trade-sensitive industries.

- **Healthcare/biotech had a notable regulatory win.** ImmunityBio said the FDA accepted a supplemental filing for expanded use of ANKTIVA, giving the company a defined catalyst with a January 2027 decision date.

- **Auto sector keeps tilting electric and premium.** Mercedes-AMG’s all-electric flagship performance sedan and Volvo’s new EV launch show legacy automakers are pushing further upscale in EVs—even as pricing and demand remain delicate.
