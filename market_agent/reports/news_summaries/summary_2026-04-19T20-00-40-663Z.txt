- **Markets may be underpricing Iran-war fallout:** Stocks rose and oil fell on signs of easing U.S.-Iran tensions, but reports suggest supply-chain disruption and infrastructure damage could still weigh on growth and energy markets.

- **Relief in energy markets is boosting risk appetite:** The reopening of the **Strait of Hormuz** helped fuel a market rally and pushed mortgage rates lower for a second straight week, improving sentiment across equities and housing.

- **Fed spotlight intensifies:** Trump’s Fed chair nominee **Kevin Warsh** is expected to face questions on how he would pursue lower interest rates, making monetary policy a central market catalyst.

- **Airlines face a strategic reckoning:** Rising jet-fuel pressure and widening financial gaps are driving consolidation talk, with **United reportedly eyeing American**, signaling possible big changes in industry structure.

- **Auto sector hit by scale pressures:** **Ford recalled 1.4 million F-150s** over a gearshift issue, while separately, U.S. car dealerships are increasingly in a **“grow or die”** environment as mega-retailers expand.

- **Berkshire is lagging the broader rally:** Even as the **S&P 500 hits record highs**, Berkshire Hathaway shares have underperformed, suggesting investors are favoring higher-beta or more cyclical plays over defensive giants.

- **Media dealmaking hits a wall:** A judge halted the **Nexstar-Tegna** TV station merger, a reminder that regulatory and legal risks remain a major obstacle to consolidation.

- **Tech-policy friction is rising:** Headlines around **Anthropic and the White House** plus broader AI political tensions show that AI is increasingly becoming a regulatory and reputational business issue, not just a growth story.

- **EV infrastructure innovation to watch:** China’s **NIO** is pushing battery-swapping as an alternative to charging, highlighting how competition in EV adoption may hinge as much on infrastructure convenience as on vehicle design.
