- **Markets sold off sharply:** The **Dow fell more than 500 points** as **tech stocks weakened** and **Treasury yields climbed**, reflecting renewed concern over inflation, rates, and geopolitical risk.

- **U.S.-China summit underwhelmed investors:** Markets had hoped for trade or tech breakthroughs, but the summit ended **without major deals**, pressuring **chip stocks** and broader risk sentiment.

- **Bond market is flashing stress:** Demand for **long-term U.S. debt weakened**, with 30-year yields around **5%**, signaling investor unease about **sticky inflation**, higher borrowing costs, and fiscal strain.

- **Oil surged on geopolitical tension:** Crude rallied strongly as **Iran-related risks** and lack of progress at the U.S.-China summit outweighed softer demand forecasts, adding to the inflation narrative.

- **AI is now hitting utility bills:** A report said wholesale power prices in parts of the **eastern U.S. jumped 76%**, driven largely by **AI data-center demand**—a sign AI’s economic impact is spreading beyond tech.

- **Microsoft got a confidence boost:** Shares rose after **Bill Ackman disclosed a new stake**, suggesting some major investors still see large-cap AI leaders as attractive despite valuation concerns.

- **OpenAI-Apple friction highlights AI partnership risks:** Reports that OpenAI felt “burned” by Apple’s ChatGPT integration point to rising tension over **distribution, branding, and product control** in the AI ecosystem.

- **Auto sector keeps recalibrating:** **Honda** is leaning harder into **hybrids** after absorbing a major EV-related loss, underscoring how legacy automakers are shifting toward more profitable transition strategies.

- **Rivian’s R2 rollout is a key test:** Opening the **R2 configurator** is an important milestone for Rivian, as the lower-priced SUV is central to whether the company can scale beyond the premium EV niche.

- **Transportation disruption is an economic watchpoint:** The **Long Island Rail Road** faces both severe service issues and a potential shutdown, a reminder that infrastructure and labor disruptions can quickly affect regional business activity.
