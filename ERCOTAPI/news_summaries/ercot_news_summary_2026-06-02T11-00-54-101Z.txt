(The Daily Caller: “How Texas Leads The New Natural Gas Pipeline Boom”)
- National coverage continues to highlight solar and batteries as major contributors to summer grid readiness; in ERCOT terms, that supports the view that incremental solar-plus-storage additions are improving reserve margins during many net-load periods, even as planners still must manage evening ramps, correlated weather risk, and localized congestion. (Mother Jones/Canary Media: “Our Power Grid Is in Better Shape This Summer, Thanks to Solar and Batteries”)
