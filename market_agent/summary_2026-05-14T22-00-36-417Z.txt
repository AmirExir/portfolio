- **Fed shift, but no easy rate cuts:** The Senate confirmed **Kevin Warsh as Fed chair**, yet markets are hearing a hawkish message: Boston Fed’s **Susan Collins said rate hikes are still possible** as inflation risks rise. Takeaway: a friendlier White House doesn’t necessarily mean easier monetary policy.

- **Bond market reprices higher inflation:** The **10-year Treasury yield hit its highest since July** after fresh inflation data, reinforcing the view that rates may stay higher for longer. That’s a headwind for rate-sensitive sectors and equity valuations.

- **Cisco underscores AI spending strength:** **Cisco beat on earnings and revenue**, with strong AI-related orders helping push the stock to record highs. It’s another sign that enterprise AI infrastructure demand remains robust.

- **AI boom meets power and public-perception constraints:** **xAI** is reportedly running nearly **50 gas turbines** at a Mississippi data center, while Gallup says **70% of Americans oppose AI data centers in their area**. The AI trade is no longer just about chips and software—it’s increasingly about energy, permitting, and local backlash.

- **Big Tech and policy continue to blur:** A billionaire-heavy delegation including **Musk, Huang, and Cook** is tied to Trump’s China meeting, highlighting how geopolitics and corporate strategy are becoming more intertwined—especially around trade, tech, and supply chains.

- **AI competition is broadening:** **Anthropic** is moving downmarket with tools for **small businesses**, while **Amazon** launched **“Alexa for Shopping.”** The next AI battleground looks less like mega-enterprise contracts and more like mass adoption and workflow integration.

- **Labor and restructuring remain in focus:** **LinkedIn** is cutting jobs and scaling back investment, a reminder that even profitable tech platforms are tightening spending while reallocating toward priority areas.

- **Transport divergence:** **Allegiant** says leisure demand is still solid as its **Sun Country acquisition** closes, but a potential **LIRR strike** could severely disrupt commuting in the New York region. Consumer travel demand is holding up, while infrastructure and labor risks linger.

- **Energy and grid stress are becoming business issues:** FERC’s chair said **PJM may be “too big to function,”** spotlighting concerns over grid governance just as data-center power demand surges. Grid bottlenecks are turning into a strategic constraint for industrial and AI growth.

- **Dealmaking and speculative themes stay hot:** **Arm/SoftBank reportedly made a late bid for Cerebras** ahead of its IPO, while **space stocks jumped** on reports of a **SpaceX-Google orbital data center** concept. Investors are still rewarding scarcity and futuristic infrastructure narratives.

- **Ford gets a new angle from Wall Street:** Shares jumped after optimism around its **CATL-backed energy storage business**, suggesting investors may increasingly value automakers for energy-adjacent businesses—not just vehicle sales.

- **Supply-chain fragility is back in view:** Reports on **Iran war-related shortages** show how quickly geopolitical disruptions can spread beyond oil into industrial inputs and consumer goods. Markets may be underestimating second-order effects.
