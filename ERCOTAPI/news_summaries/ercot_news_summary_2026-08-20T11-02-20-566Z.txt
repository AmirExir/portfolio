- ERCOT posted NPRR1346 to double the transportation-cost adder embedded in the Fuel Oil Price from $0.05/gal to $0.10/gal, a small but targeted change that could improve compensation for oil-fired or dual-fuel generation during tight fuel conditions and emergency operations. (NPRR1346/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1345 to extend the Exceptional Fuel Cost process sunset from April 1, 2027 to April 1, 2030, signaling ERCOT expects fuel-cost stress and uplift treatment to remain a live reliability and market-design issue for several more years. (NPRR1345/ERCOT)
- ERCOT’s PRS report for NPRR1342 shows recommended approval of a new Mitigated Offer Cap process for Contract for Capacity resources, an important step in aligning reliability procurements with offer mitigation rules so out-of-market capacity can participate without distorting pricing or creating unclear bidding incentives. (1342NPRR-08 PRS Report 081226/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1343 to add a bid fee for inefficient hourly PTP Obligation bids in the Day-Ahead Market, a market-efficiency reform aimed at reducing low-value congestion trading behavior and improving DAM quality for hedgers and congestion price formation. (NPRR1343/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1344 to move the Texas Market Test Plan from an Other Binding Document into Protocol Section 19, tightening governance around market testing and making implementation approvals more standardized as ERCOT continues rolling out complex market and system changes. (NPRR1344/ERCOT)
- Large-load policy remains a major ERCOT focus: PGRR145 would create a “Batch Zero” process to study mature large-load interconnection requests on a system-wide basis, while PGRR144 tightens dynamic model submission requirements for large loads including large computational loads; together these changes point to more centralized screening of data center and other large-load impacts on voltage stability, transfer capability, and upgrade timing. (PGRR145 and PGRR144/ERCOT)
- ERCOT also posted new PGRR145 comment material that was not fully summarized here, but the title indicates fresh ERCOT comments on the Batch Zero large-load process—likely significant because implementation details will shape which projects qualify, how system-wide studies are sequenced, and how quickly transmission upgrades are identified. (145PGRR-45 ERCOT Comments 041726/ERCOT)
- NOGRR289 would let certain Large Computational Loads meet ride-through requirements up to 365 days after in-service where cooling-load issues apply, easing near-term interconnection constraints for some data-center-type loads but potentially extending the period during which ERCOT must manage non-standard disturbance performance risk. (NOGRR289/ERCOT)
- NOGRR288 would add Energy Storage Resources to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service, broadening the tools ERCOT can use for reliability reserve deployment and further integrating batteries into reliability operations rather than energy arbitrage alone. (NOGRR288/ERCOT)
- NOGRR287 would require TO/TDSP coordination to identify and minimize overlap of essential loads in load-shed blocks, a governance and emergency-operations improvement that could make rotating outages more effective by preserving more interruptible load when firm load shed is needed. (NOGRR287/ERCOT)
- NOGRR286 would add 765-kV operational voltage limits, a notable transmission-operability milestone that suggests ERCOT is preparing formal operating criteria for extra-high-voltage infrastructure with implications for future backbone expansion, transfer capability, and voltage-control practices. (NOGRR286/ERCOT)
- Planning and interconnection transparency updates continue: PGRR147 aligns allowable non-consequential load loss treatment with NERC contingency standards, PGRR146 reduces stability assessment frequency for planned large generators and large loads from quarterly to every four months, PGRR132 clarifies new resources must use a new SGIA to interconnect, and PGRR131 adds mandatory interconnection cost reporting—collectively signaling more formalized planning compliance and clearer cost/accountability rules for new supply and load entrants. (PGRR147, PGRR146, PGRR132, and PGRR131/ERCOT)
