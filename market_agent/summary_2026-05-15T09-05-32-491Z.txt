- **Fed politics intensify, but cuts aren’t imminent:** The Senate confirmed **Kevin Warsh as Fed chair**, yet the broader message from Washington and Fed officials is still **higher-for-longer**. Boston Fed’s Susan Collins even flagged a **possible rate-hike scenario** if inflation worsens.

- **Markets hit records on AI-fueled tech strength:** The **S&P 500 and Nasdaq reached new highs**, driven mainly by big tech and AI names. Futures were little changed afterward, suggesting enthusiasm remains strong but narrow.

- **Bond market sends a tougher inflation warning:** Investors accepted **5% yields on 30-year Treasuries for the first time since 2007**, a sign that markets are demanding more compensation for persistent inflation and energy-related risks.

- **Oil is stable, but the backdrop is fragile:** Crude held steady ahead of **Trump-Xi talks**, while tensions tied to **Iran and the Strait of Hormuz** kept geopolitical risk elevated. The market appears calm, but energy remains a major macro wildcard.

- **AI boom keeps attracting capital:** **Cerebras raised its IPO price** to target **$5.5 billion**, valuing the chipmaker at about **$40 billion**—another sign that investor appetite for AI infrastructure is still very strong.

- **Tech spending is shifting toward AI—even with layoffs:** **Cisco posted record revenue** but will cut about **4,000 jobs** as it reallocates spending to **AI, security, and optics**. Likewise, **LinkedIn** is pairing layoffs with operational changes. The pattern: healthy top lines don’t guarantee headcount stability.

- **Nvidia gets another geopolitical boost:** **Nvidia shares rallied** as CEO **Jensen Huang joined Trump’s China trip**, reinforcing how closely chip stocks are tied to both AI demand and U.S.-China policy signals.

- **Airline consolidation continues in a difficult budget-travel market:** **Allegiant completed its acquisition of Sun Country**, creating a larger low-cost carrier as discount airlines face pressure from costs and uneven travel demand.

- **Crypto legislation may gain momentum:** Sen. **John Kennedy’s support** for a Senate crypto bill removes a key uncertainty, improving the odds of further movement on digital-asset regulation.

- **AI infrastructure faces growing scrutiny:** TechCrunch reports **xAI is running nearly 50 gas turbines** at a Mississippi data center under a regulatory loophole—highlighting the rising tension between **AI expansion, power demand, and environmental oversight**.
