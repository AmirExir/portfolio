- **Rates may stay higher for longer:** Fed minutes showed officials remain open to **further rate hikes if inflation stays sticky**, keeping pressure on borrowing costs, valuations, and rate-sensitive sectors.

- **Energy offered some relief:** **U.S. crude fell below $100** after Trump said Iran talks were in their final stages, which could ease inflation pressure if sustained.

- **Consumers look more fragile:** E.l.f. Beauty plans to **reverse some tariff-driven price hikes** after seeing demand weaken, suggesting price sensitivity is rising amid broader household strain.

- **Retail had a bright spot:** **Target posted its strongest sales growth in four years**, indicating that turnaround efforts under its new CEO may be gaining traction.

- **Labor tension in chips eased—for now:** Samsung reached a tentative deal with workers, helping avert a strike during a **global memory shortage** and reducing near-term supply chain risk.

- **Infrastructure got a major public-sector milestone:** Amtrak and the U.S. Transportation Department named **Halmar and Skanska** as master developers for the New York Penn Station renovation, advancing a high-profile transit redevelopment.
