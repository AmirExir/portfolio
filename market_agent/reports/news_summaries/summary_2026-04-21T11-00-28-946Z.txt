- **Geopolitics is driving markets lower:** U.S.-Iran tensions escalated, pushing **Dow futures down more than 350 points** and reviving a broad risk-off tone across global markets.

- **Oil is the clearest immediate winner:** Fresh disruptions tied to the **Strait of Hormuz** sent crude prices sharply higher, underscoring how quickly conflict can feed into **energy inflation and consumer fuel costs**.

- **Crypto is wobbling, but less than oil and equities:** **Bitcoin, ether, and solana fell**, though bitcoin’s relatively modest decline suggests it’s behaving more like a **high-volatility macro asset** than a pure panic trade.

- **China’s clean-energy leverage is growing:** A Politico analysis says the Iran war may further strengthen **Beijing’s dominance in clean-tech supply chains**, creating a strategic dilemma: faster decarbonization may mean deeper dependence on China.

- **Solar hit a major milestone globally:** The IEA says **solar led global energy growth in 2025**, a sign that electrification demand from **EVs and data centers** is becoming a major business and infrastructure theme.

- **China kept rates unchanged:** Beijing left benchmark lending rates steady for an 11th straight month, signaling policymakers see **less urgency for stimulus** even as Middle East risks rise.

- **Stablecoins are drawing systemic scrutiny:** Central bankers warned that growing use of **U.S. dollar-backed stablecoins** could intensify **dollarization risk in emerging markets** and create regulatory headaches.

- **Big biotech dealmaking remains active:** **Eli Lilly** is reportedly nearing a **$2B+ acquisition of Kelonia Therapeutics**, highlighting continued appetite for targeted biotech M&A despite a choppy macro backdrop.

- **Prediction-market boom continues:** **Polymarket** is reportedly discussing a new raise at around a **$15B valuation**, showing investors still see strong upside in alternative trading/speculation platforms.

- **Cybersecurity risk remains a boardroom issue:** **Vercel disclosed a breach** tied to a third-party compromise, another reminder that vendor and identity-chain vulnerabilities remain a serious enterprise risk.

- **Consumer-product trust took a hit in Europe:** **HiPP recalled baby food jars in Austria** after samples tested positive for rat poison, a headline likely to sharpen attention on **food safety and brand risk management**.
