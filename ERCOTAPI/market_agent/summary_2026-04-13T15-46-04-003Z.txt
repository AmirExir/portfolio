- Texas is providing data centers more than $1 billion per year in sales-tax breaks, according to Texas Tribune reporting highlighted by Grist and TechRadar. For ERCOT, that matters because state policy is continuing to accelerate very large new load additions even as grid planners are already grappling with AI, cloud, and crypto-related demand growth. (The Texas Tribune/Grist/TechRadar)

- Multiple reports point to another wave of massive Texas data-center development, including Aligned Data Centers’ 540 MW “Project Caprock” in Northwest Texas and broader investor focus on Texas AI campuses, Permian-powered load growth, and Abilene-area buildouts. The core ERCOT implication is rising pressure on transmission, interconnection queues, local deliverability, and the need for firm supply arrangements as hyperscale loads cluster in specific regions. (GlobeNewswire/Business Insider/IBTimes.com.au)

- The broader AI infrastructure boom is increasingly tied to natural gas and pipeline expansion, with Fortune and OilPrice describing growing gas-fired power demand from data centers while other outlets note project delays from power costs, equipment shortages, and siting resistance. For ERCOT, that combination suggests a tougher planning environment where load growth may outpace near-term infrastructure readiness, increasing the value of dispatchable generation, transmission upgrades, and clearer governance around large-load interconnection and cost allocation. (Fortune/OilPrice/TechRadar/Harvard Gazette)
