- **Geopolitics lifted oil and markets:** Oil rose after renewed **U.S.-Iran fire exchanges near the Strait of Hormuz**, while U.S. stock futures edged higher as investors balanced Middle East risk against hopes around the upcoming **April jobs report**.

- **Jobs report is the macro focal point:** Traders are bracing for a **soft U.S. payrolls print** (consensus around 55,000 jobs, unemployment seen at 4.3%), making labor data the key near-term driver for rates and equities.

- **AI chip controls face a new stress test:** Bloomberg reported U.S. suspicions that **advanced Nvidia chips embedded in Super Micro servers were smuggled to China via Thailand**, with **Alibaba** among end customers—highlighting how export restrictions may be getting routed around.

- **White House signals lighter-touch AI policy:** The administration is reportedly leaning toward **“partnership” over stricter regulation** for AI companies, a market-friendly stance that could support continued private-sector AI investment.

- **AI spending remains strong, but costs are rising:**  
  - **CoreWeave** topped revenue estimates as AI cloud demand surged, but raised the low end of capex guidance due to **higher component costs**.  
  - **IREN** rallied on a new **Nvidia partnership**, underscoring how former crypto infrastructure players are pivoting into AI.

- **AI is also driving corporate restructuring:** **Cloudflare** plans to cut **1,100+ jobs globally** as it reorganizes around an **AI-first** model—another sign AI is boosting efficiency expectations but pressuring headcount.

- **Mixed tech earnings tape:**  
  - **Datadog** jumped after better-than-expected results, a bright spot in software.  
  - **Trade Desk** fell sharply on an earnings miss and weak guidance.  
  - **Coinbase** swung to a loss and posted another revenue decline, showing continued sensitivity to crypto-market weakness.

- **Aerospace and defense momentum continues:**  
  - **Airbus** won a **$19 billion AirAsia order** for 150 planes.  
  - **Boeing** got an update tied to its **Air Force One** contract.  
  - **HawkEye 360** surged in its IPO debut, reflecting strong investor appetite for **space and defense-linked assets**.

- **Supply-chain shock hits agriculture:** California growers are reportedly set to destroy **420,000 peach trees** after **Del Monte’s canning closures**, a stark example of how processor shutdowns can ripple through farm economics.

- **Transit megaproject finances under strain:** **Sound Transit** outlined plans to manage a **$34.5 billion funding shortfall** over two decades, signaling potential cuts, delays, or redesigns for major infrastructure expansion.
