- **Spirit Airlines collapses:** Spirit said it will shut down immediately after a White House rescue plan fell apart — a major blow for the U.S. low-cost travel market and a sign that weaker consumer-facing businesses are struggling to secure lifelines.

- **Berkshire posts strong earnings:** Berkshire Hathaway’s profit more than doubled, driven by insurance, rail, and energy — reinforcing how diversified, cash-generative “old economy” businesses are still outperforming in a volatile environment.

- **Markets keep climbing despite caution:** The Nasdaq and S&P reportedly hit fresh milestones, suggesting investors are still rewarding earnings resilience and mega-cap strength even as macro risks remain elevated.

- **Economic split is widening:** New research highlighted a more entrenched **K-shaped economy**, with lower-income households hit harder by inflation while wealthier households continue benefiting from asset gains.

- **Big Tech deepens defense ties:** Nvidia, Microsoft, and AWS are expanding AI use on classified Pentagon networks — another sign that defense and government contracts are becoming a meaningful growth lane for enterprise AI.

- **Crypto regulation inches forward:** Draft “Clarity Act” language would allow some stablecoin rewards while restricting products that resemble bank deposits — a step toward clearer rules, but one that still tries to protect traditional banking turf.

- **Oil and gas price pressure builds:** Rising fuel prices in the U.S., especially in places like Illinois, reflect geopolitical tensions and refinery disruptions; energy remains a key inflation risk for consumers and businesses alike.

- **Ford emphasizes affordability:** CEO Jim Farley said future Ford models will skew more affordable, signaling automakers are adjusting to consumer budget stress rather than betting solely on premium pricing.

- **UAE’s strategic shift from oil:** A Forbes analysis argues the UAE’s sovereign wealth has grown so large that it now has less need to align tightly with OPEC — underscoring how Gulf economies are increasingly leveraging capital, not just crude.

- **Healthcare consolidation watch:** Atrium Health’s proposed combination with WakeMed would expand one of North Carolina’s largest hospital systems, likely drawing scrutiny over pricing power and regional competition.
