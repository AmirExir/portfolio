- EIA now forecasts utility-scale solar generation in ERCOT will exceed coal generation in 2026 for the first time, confirming a structural fuel-mix shift rather than a seasonal anomaly. The change points to deeper midday price suppression, faster coal displacement, and greater operational dependence on flexible thermal units, storage, and transmission to manage evening ramps and reliability risk. This also raises planning pressure around ancillary services, curtailment management, and resource adequacy in a solar-heavy system. (EIA)

- Large-load growth remains the clearest emerging ERCOT issue: multiple reports point to accelerating AI/data-center expansion in Texas, including a new ~6 MW South Texas modular AI deployment and broader North Texas project pushback. For ERCOT, the key implication is not the individual MW but the cumulative effect on interconnection queues, substation and transmission upgrades, deliverability, and local reliability if clusters form faster than grid infrastructure can be built. Community opposition and local moratoria suggest siting/permitting could become a meaningful constraint on load realization assumptions. (PRNewswire; Insurance Journal; Dallas Observer)

- Hill County’s one-year moratorium on new data centers and energy storage is notable because it shows Texas local governments are beginning to resist large new power-intensive development. Near term, that could delay some ERCOT large-load additions and battery siting in specific areas; longer term, it adds a non-grid bottleneck to ERCOT planning, where forecast load growth may not materialize on the timeline assumed in transmission and resource studies. (Insurance Journal)

- CleanSpark said it has 585 MW of ERCOT-approved capacity, highlighting that crypto-linked flexible load continues to secure meaningful positions on the Texas grid. The grid relevance is two-sided: these loads can intensify local capacity and transmission needs when energized, but can also provide operational flexibility if they curtail during scarcity events, making their performance during stress periods increasingly important for ERCOT reliability assessments. (PRNewswire)

- Canadian Solar’s quarterly update is broadly relevant to ERCOT because continued high module shipments support the pipeline economics behind Texas solar and solar-plus-storage additions. While not an ERCOT filing, ongoing global supply availability lowers one barrier to new build execution, reinforcing expectations for more renewable capacity seeking interconnection, transmission access, and congestion relief in ERCOT zones. (PRNewswire)

- Fervo’s high-profile geothermal financing and scaling ambitions add to the longer-term conversation about firm clean capacity that could eventually matter in ERCOT, especially as solar penetration rises and evening reliability needs grow. Geothermal is not an immediate Texas grid solution here, but investor appetite for 24/7 clean generation underscores the market need ERCOT is creating for dispatchable, non-weather-dependent resources. (Fortune)

- AEP’s equity offering is not ERCOT-specific on its face, but it signals continued utility capital raising to fund large grid investment programs amid load growth and infrastructure needs. For Texas and ERCOT-adjacent service territories, this financing backdrop supports the broader theme that transmission, distribution, and interconnection capital requirements are rising with electrification and large commercial load expansion. (PRNewswire)

- Texas awarded USA Rare Earth a $14.2 million Semiconductor Innovation Fund grant for its Round Top project, which is more industrial-policy than immediate ERCOT news but still relevant for future load and infrastructure planning in West Texas. If processing and related manufacturing scale, these projects can add incremental industrial demand in areas already shaped by transmission constraints, renewable buildout, and oil-and-gas electrification. (GlobeNewswire)
