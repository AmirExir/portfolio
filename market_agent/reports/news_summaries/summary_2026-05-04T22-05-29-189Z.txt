- **Oil is the biggest macro story today:** OPEC+ agreed only a modest output increase while disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz continue, keeping crude markets tight and raising the risk of higher fuel costs for businesses and consumers.

- **The U.S. is emerging as a key global energy backstop:** Reports indicate surging tanker demand for U.S. oil exports as buyers look to replace disrupted Middle East supply—supportive for U.S. producers, but a reminder that global energy security remains fragile.

- **AI’s commercial promise in healthcare got a notable boost:** A Harvard-linked study found AI outperformed two human doctors in emergency-room diagnosis scenarios, strengthening the investment case for medical AI—though regulation and trust remain big hurdles.

- **Big Tech earnings reinforced an AI divide:** Results suggest investors are increasingly separating AI infrastructure and platform winners from companies still struggling to show clear monetization.

- **Labor policy is starting to catch up with AI adoption:** A Chinese court ruled companies can’t simply fire workers because AI automates their role, signaling that legal constraints on AI-led restructuring may tighten globally.

- **Berkshire Hathaway passed an important leadership test:** Greg Abel received broadly positive reviews in his first annual meeting as the visible leader, helping reassure investors about continuity after Warren Buffett.

- **Airline sector stress remains elevated:** Spirit’s failed push for government help underscores how fragile the low-cost carrier model has become, while United faced fresh operational and security incidents at Newark—adding to scrutiny on airline resilience and risk management.

- **Consumer value still matters in food retail:** Costco’s return of a lower-cost ready-to-eat meal kit highlights how retailers are leaning into convenience-plus-affordability as households remain price sensitive.

- **Restaurant operators continue to face local operating risk:** Rodney Scott’s BBQ closing a Charleston site “until further notice” is a small but telling reminder that city-level costs, compliance, and profitability pressures remain intense for hospitality businesses.

- **Energy policy uncertainty is widening beyond oil:** The Trump administration’s reported national-security-based clampdown on wind projects adds another layer of regulatory risk for renewable developers and investors.
