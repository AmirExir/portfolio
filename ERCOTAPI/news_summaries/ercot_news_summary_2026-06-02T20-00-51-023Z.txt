- The grid impact of the proposed large-load evaluation changes is significant: ERCOT is signaling that multi-gigawatt data center demand cannot be treated as ordinary load growth, and that deliverability, timing, and system upgrade needs will increasingly determine which projects advance. (Slashdot citing ERCOT large-load interconnection discussion)
- For planning and reliability, the emerging large-load framework implies tighter scrutiny of transmission readiness, generation adequacy, and interconnection milestones, likely affecting how developers secure capacity, how planners model load growth, and how ERCOT manages speculative requests in high-growth zones. (Slashdot citing ERCOT large-load interconnection discussion)
