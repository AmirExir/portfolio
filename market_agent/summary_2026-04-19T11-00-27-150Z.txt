- **Media deal hits legal wall:** A federal judge blocked Nexstar’s **$6.2B acquisition of Tegna** pending an antitrust case, underscoring tougher scrutiny of consolidation in local media.

- **AI leadership shakeups continue:** **OpenAI lost three top executives** as it narrows focus and trims “side quests,” a sign of mounting pressure to commercialize core products amid stronger competition.

- **Anthropic gains strategic momentum:** The AI startup appears to be **rebuilding ties with the Trump administration** while also expanding product ambitions, highlighting how AI firms are becoming increasingly intertwined with government and enterprise priorities.

- **Meta cuts deepen:** Reports say **Meta may lay off about 8,000 employees in May**, reinforcing that even mega-cap tech firms are still in efficiency mode despite the AI boom.

- **Bankruptcy in legacy retail media:** **QVC Group filed for Chapter 11**, a notable casualty of cord-cutting and e-commerce competition as old TV-based retail models struggle to stay relevant.

- **Energy shock hits airlines:** Ongoing **jet fuel shortages and elevated fuel costs** are pressuring airlines globally, with **Air Canada suspending JFK service** and **Spirit reportedly seeking government aid**—a warning sign for fares, routes, and profitability.

- **Oil relief may be temporary:** Oil and gasoline prices eased after signs the **Strait of Hormuz may remain open**, but markets remain fragile; any renewed disruption could quickly reverse the drop.

- **American Airlines rules out big M&A:** The carrier said it **won’t pursue a merger with United**, suggesting management sees regulatory and operational hurdles as too high for mega-airline consolidation.

- **“AI pivot” fever spreads beyond tech:** Axios notes companies are increasingly **rebranding around AI** to excite investors—echoing the dot-com era and raising fresh questions about hype versus fundamentals.

- **Consumer identity tech expands:** **Sam Altman’s World** is pushing deeper into partnerships, starting with **Tinder**, signaling growing commercial interest in human-verification tools as AI blurs online authenticity.
