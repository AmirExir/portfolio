- **Markets wobble near highs as bond yields jump:** Rising Treasury yields erased much of the week’s stock gains, hit small caps especially hard, and put the spotlight on upcoming **Nvidia earnings** as a key market test.

- **AI is reshaping hiring—and not evenly:** New reporting suggests companies may increasingly favor **mid- and senior-level workers** for AI-driven productivity, while data also show **early signs of declines in AI-exposed roles**, especially entry-level and routine jobs.

- **Energy markets face a geopolitical stress point:** Oil analysts warn of a possible **sharp, “non-linear” price spike** in June if supply disruptions persist around the **Strait of Hormuz**, raising risks for inflation and transport-heavy sectors.

- **Big money keeps crowding into AI leaders:**  
  - **Nvidia** is drawing fresh analyst attention ahead of earnings.  
  - **Microsoft** gained backing from **Pershing Square**, highlighting a split among major investors over AI spending—but continued conviction in platform winners.  
  - **Cerebras**’ blockbuster IPO underscores how strong investor appetite remains for AI-chip plays despite high execution risk.

- **India’s chip ambitions got a boost:** **ASML and Tata Electronics** are partnering to advance India’s semiconductor manufacturing capabilities—another sign of countries pushing to localize strategic tech supply chains.

- **Space and defense remain active capital themes:**  
  - **Northrop Grumman** won a **$398 million Space Force satellite contract**, reinforcing demand for secure military communications.  
  - Separately, **BlackRock** is reportedly considering a **multibillion-dollar investment in a potential SpaceX IPO**, signaling sustained appetite for large-scale aerospace bets.

- **Consumer and retail headlines were mixed:** A major **pet supplies franchisee filed for Chapter 11**, pointing to ongoing pressure in parts of retail, while **early Memorial Day promotions** suggest e-commerce players are leaning hard into demand stimulation.

- **Operational and product risk surfaced in consumer staples and transit:**  
  - **Straus Family Creamery** recalled ice cream sold in 17 states over possible **metal contamination**.  
  - A **Long Island Rail Road strike** shut down the busiest U.S. commuter rail line, highlighting labor friction and economic disruption risk in major metros.

- **Governance and politics remain a market backdrop:** Fresh scrutiny around **Trump family investment disclosures** adds to the broader theme that political and reputational risks continue to intersect with business headlines.
