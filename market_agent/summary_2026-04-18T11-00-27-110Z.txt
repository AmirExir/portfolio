- **Netflix tumbles on outlook, leadership shift:** Shares fell about 10% premarket after Q2 profit guidance missed expectations; co-founder Reed Hastings is also exiting, adding to investor unease despite a Q1 beat.

- **Markets cautious as geopolitics dominate sentiment:** U.S. stock futures were mixed and most Asia markets fell as investors weighed signs of de-escalation in the Middle East against lingering uncertainty.

- **Energy risk remains a major macro overhang:** Even with talk of an Iran deal and a ceasefire, the supply picture looks fragile—IEA warnings suggest oil and gas recovery could take up to two years, while Europe may face jet-fuel strain if Gulf flows are disrupted.

- **AI race keeps widening into new industries:** OpenAI launched a life-sciences model aimed at speeding drug research, while Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7—further evidence that AI competition is expanding beyond software into biotech and enterprise workflows.

- **Retail and consumer shakeups continue:** QVC filed for Chapter 11 but expects to keep operating, while Walmart is investing in store remodels and faster delivery—showing a split between distressed legacy retail and better-capitalized omnichannel players.

- **Logistics and transport are being reshaped:** Canada Post is moving more homes from door-to-door delivery to community mailboxes, and Lufthansa reportedly shut down CityLine, underscoring pressure to cut costs in legacy networks.

- **Air travel is experimenting with product innovation:** Air New Zealand plans lie-flat bunk beds for economy passengers on long-haul routes, a notable attempt to create premium-style upsells without redesigning the whole cabin.

- **Healthcare policy is creating niche winners:** Hims & Hers shares got a boost from expectations that peptide policy changes could open a new growth path as its GLP-1 business evolves.

- **Tech sector job pressure persists:** Snap is cutting nearly 100 Seattle-area workers, a reminder that even as AI investment surges, broader tech employment remains uneven.

- **A cautionary note on “AI pivots”:** Allbirds’ stock reversed sharply after enthusiasm over its AI-infrastructure pivot faded, suggesting markets are becoming less willing to reward superficial AI rebranding.
