- **Oil back in focus:** Crude jumped about 4% after Trump rejected Iran’s response to a ceasefire proposal, reviving geopolitical risk and weighing on stock futures despite recent Wall Street records.

- **Markets sending mixed signals:** Asian equities were mixed, U.S. futures slipped, and investors are balancing strong momentum in stocks against rising energy prices and Middle East uncertainty.

- **Big oil traders cash in:** Europe’s oil majors reportedly made up to **$4.75 billion** from trading the volatility around the Iran war, highlighting how market dislocation is creating outsized profits for sophisticated energy desks.

- **China is stabilizing, but at a cost:** Beijing fixed the yuan at a **3-year high** ahead of a Trump-Xi meeting, while factory-gate inflation hit its fastest pace since the pandemic as war-related energy costs fed through supply chains.

- **South Korea gets a bullish upgrade:** JPMorgan lifted its upside target for the **Kospi to 10,000**, citing a memory-chip rebound, governance reforms, and industrial growth—another sign AI and semiconductors remain central to the Asia equity story.

- **AI boom is still red-hot:** Cerebras is reportedly set to raise its IPO price range amid strong demand, while OpenAI jobs are being portrayed as some of the most lucrative “lottery tickets” of the current tech cycle.

- **U.S. labor sentiment looks unusually weak:** A Gallup/Axios report says America has the world’s largest generational gap in job-market optimism, suggesting headline resilience may be masking deeper confidence issues.

- **Fed and market tension remains:** Commentary around Jerome Powell’s legacy underscores an unresolved battle against inflation and political pressure—especially relevant now as oil rises threaten to complicate the inflation outlook again.

- **On Wall Street, the debate is widening:** Bulls like Ed Yardeni see the **S&P 500** climbing to **8,250** on resilient earnings, while skeptics such as Michael Burry are invoking historic warning signs on valuations.

- **Worth watching:** Reports of suspiciously well-timed **$7 billion oil trades** are raising insider-trading concerns, a reminder that geopolitical volatility can bring not just profit opportunities, but regulatory scrutiny too.
