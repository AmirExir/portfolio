- **HSBC Appoints New Chairman:** Brendan Nelson has been named chairman of HSBC after a year-long search, amidst challenges of balancing the bank's operations between the U.K. and Asia. His appointment highlights the complexities of leadership in global finance.

- **Amazon Unveils AI Initiatives:** At the AWS re:Invent 2025 conference, Amazon announced the launch of “AI Factories,” allowing businesses and governments to deploy AI systems within their own data centers, marking a significant move in the competitive AI landscape.

- **Prada Acquires Versace:** The Prada Group has finalized its $1.375 billion acquisition of Versace, aiming to combine the contrasting fashion styles of both brands and strengthen its position in the luxury fashion market.
