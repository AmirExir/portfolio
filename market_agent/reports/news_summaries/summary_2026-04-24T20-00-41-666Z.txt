- **Markets pulled back from highs:** U.S. stocks closed lower as **software shares sold off** and **oil jumped** amid renewed Middle East tension, showing investors are getting more sensitive to valuation and geopolitical risk.

- **Energy risk moved to center stage:** The **IEA warned the Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz disruption** may be the biggest energy security threat ever, reinforcing inflation and supply-chain concerns if oil stays elevated.

- **Big Tech cost cuts continue despite AI spending boom:**  
  - **Meta** plans to cut **8,000 jobs** while ramping up AI investment.  
  - **Nike** is cutting **1,400 roles**, mostly in tech.  
  - **Microsoft** is offering voluntary retirement to thousands.  
  **Takeaway:** companies are still trimming legacy costs to fund AI and protect margins.

- **AI investment is helping some stocks, hurting others:**  
  - **OpenAI launched GPT-5.5**, pushing further toward a broader AI platform strategy.  
  - **Microsoft shares fell** as investors focused on the scale of its overseas AI infrastructure spending.  
  - A reported **leak of Anthropic’s “Mythos” model** raised fresh AI security concerns.

- **Intel delivered a rare bright spot in chips:** **Intel stock surged after a Q1 beat and strong outlook**, suggesting investors are rewarding credible signs of operational improvement and near-term demand resilience.

- **Software sentiment remains fragile:** Even decent results from **IBM and ServiceNow** triggered a sector selloff, a sign that investors now want **clear AI monetization and strong guidance**, not just “better than feared” numbers.

- **Healthcare saw a landmark biotech milestone:** The **FDA approved the first gene therapy aimed at restoring hearing**, a potentially major breakthrough for biotech and a reminder that innovation stories still matter beyond AI.

- **Consumer finance and retail updates:**  
  - **Capital One will pay $425 million** in a savings-account settlement, adding to pressure on banks around deposit practices.  
  - **Starbucks says loyalty changes are attracting value-conscious customers**, a useful signal that consumers remain price-sensitive but responsive to targeted rewards.

- **Politics and business are colliding more directly:**  
  - **Citadel may halt its NYC expansion** amid a clash with local political leadership.  
  - A **White House drug-pricing deal with Regeneron** points to continued pressure on pharma pricing.  
  **Bottom line:** regulation and political rhetoric are increasingly material business risks.
