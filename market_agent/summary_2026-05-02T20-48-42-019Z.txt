- **Markets mixed, but tech still leads:** The **S&P 500 and Nasdaq hit fresh records**, supported by strong tech earnings, while the **Dow lagged** as investors balanced upbeat corporate results against geopolitical and tariff risks.

- **Apple helped steady sentiment:** **Apple’s strong forecast** reassured investors that big tech demand remains resilient, even as it warned about **higher memory-chip costs** and ongoing **Mac shortages**.

- **Trade tensions resurfaced:** Stocks gave up some gains after **Trump threatened higher tariffs on European vehicles**, a reminder that policy risk is still a live market variable.

- **Oil shock is spilling into consumers and travel:** The **Iran war-driven fuel spike** is pressuring households and businesses; **Michigan gas prices** are nearing records, and **Spirit Airlines** may need government support as the fuel crisis hits weaker carriers first.

- **Fed outlook turned murkier:** Attention remains on the **unusual dissent at the Fed**, with some officials pushing back on language implying the **next move would be a rate cut**—a sign rate relief may be less certain than markets hoped.

- **AI’s defense business is accelerating:** The **Pentagon signed classified-network deals with major AI firms** including Microsoft, Amazon, and Google, underscoring how quickly AI is becoming embedded in national-security spending.

- **Meta faces regulatory pressure on multiple fronts:** Despite strong recent business performance, Meta is battling legal and policy risk; it is **threatening to pull Facebook and Instagram from New Mexico** over child-safety requirements.

- **Biotech reminder: pipeline risk cuts both ways:** A setback for a **lung-cancer treatment seen as a potential Merck rival** triggered a sharp selloff, highlighting how fast sentiment can reverse in drug development.

- **Currency markets remain on edge:** After **Japan’s $34.5 billion intervention**, traders are watching for more action to support the yen—another signal that volatility is extending beyond equities.

- **Consumer product liability still matters:** **Thermos recalled 8 million products** after stopper defects caused serious injuries, a reminder that operational and legal risks can quickly become material business events.
