What to watch:
- NVDA — mixed; watch for any confirmed DOJ or congressional action. A formal investigation, board statement, or customer fallout would increase downside risk; official clearance would improve the setup.
- AI chip supply chain — bearish if Megaspeed restrictions spread to more intermediaries. New export-control names or disclosed customer disruptions would matter most.
- HPE — mixed-to-bullish if raised guidance is backed by sustained orders and margins. Evidence of backlog quality or repeat guidance increases would strengthen the case.
- MU — bearish if oversupply concerns start appearing in company commentary or peer results. Tighter supply discipline or stable pricing data would challenge that view.
