For Texas, that likely means more proposals involving onsite generation, hybrid supply, and nontraditional load interconnection structures. (PRNewswire)

- The growing focus on residential batteries and virtual power plants is not ERCOT-specific in this coverage, but it is directionally important for Texas: aggregated behind-the-meter storage could become a more material reliability tool for peak shaving, ancillary-type services, and distribution support as summer peaks and large-load growth intensify. (Yale.edu)

The takeaway for planning is a stronger premium on resources that perform during correlated low-wind/high-load hours. (Naturalnews.com)
