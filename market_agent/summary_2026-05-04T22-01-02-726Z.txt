- **Oil is the biggest macro story:** OPEC+ agreed only a modest output increase, while ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption is tightening global supply. The result: **higher energy-price risk, inflation pressure, and stronger reliance on U.S. exports**.

- **Airlines face a rough operating backdrop:** United was hit by two separate Newark-related incidents — one flight struck a light pole on landing and another involved an alleged passenger assault and cockpit-access attempt. **Safety, disruption, and reputational risks remain front and center for carriers.**

- **Budget aviation stress is deepening:** Reporting suggests **Spirit’s shutdown/bailout maneuvering** has intensified pressure on the low-cost airline sector, with rivals also seeking support. **Cheap-fare competition may shrink if weaker carriers disappear.**

- **Berkshire’s leadership transition looks stable:** Greg Abel received **largely positive early reviews** from Berkshire shareholders after his first annual meeting as the top executive. **Investors appear reassured on continuity, even without Buffett’s presence.**

- **AI’s business split is getting clearer:** Big Tech earnings still support the **AI boom**, but markets are increasingly distinguishing between **real monetization winners and companies merely spending heavily to keep up**.

- **AI in healthcare gained a major credibility boost:** A Harvard-linked study found an AI system outperformed two human doctors in emergency-room diagnosis scenarios. **That strengthens the case for clinical AI adoption, though regulation and trust will matter.**

- **China pushed back on AI-driven job displacement:** A Chinese court ruled companies **can’t simply fire workers because AI can replace them**. **That’s a notable signal that labor protections may become a bigger constraint on automation.**

- **U.S. wind projects face policy headwinds:** The Trump administration is reportedly using **national-security grounds to slow wind farm approvals**, stalling many projects. **That raises regulatory risk for renewable developers and investors.**

- **Consumer value still wins in food retail:** Costco’s return of a low-cost ready-to-eat meal kit underscores how **convenience plus affordability** continues to resonate as households remain price-conscious.

- **Local restaurant brands aren’t immune:** Rodney Scott’s BBQ closed its Charleston King Street location **until further notice**, a reminder that even high-profile food businesses face **operational and economic strain**.
