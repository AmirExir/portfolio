- Coherent broke ground on a major Sherman expansion for lasers, optical components, and compound semiconductors used in AI infrastructure. While not a direct ERCOT filing, the project reinforces North Texas as a growing high-load industrial and data-center corridor with likely incremental transmission and large-load planning needs in the Sherman/Denison area. (Nvidia; TechPowerUp; AP)

- Multiple reports point to accelerating Texas AI and digital infrastructure buildout—from Coherent in Sherman to DataBank’s Red Oak financing and a proposed $10 billion AI data center near Ross/Lacy Lakeview—signaling sustained large-load growth pressure on ERCOT interconnection queues, substation capacity, and local transmission upgrades. (PR Newswire; Fortune; Nvidia)

- Circe Energy said it is advancing a behind-the-meter AI infrastructure platform with 2 GW of generation capacity out of Houston, underscoring a key ERCOT trend: large loads pursuing self-supply or hybrid grid arrangements to reduce time-to-power risk and avoid waiting on full grid deliverability. This could ease some peak exposure for ERCOT but complicates outage coordination, fuel assurance, and planning visibility. (PR Newswire)

- The emerging policy and market issue behind several AI-power stories is cost allocation: as very large new loads seek faster service, the practical ERCOT question is who funds the required generation, transmission, and interconnection upgrades. Expect this to remain a major governance and market-design pressure point for load interconnection standards and system planning. (Fortune; OilPrice; Reuters/The Times of India)
