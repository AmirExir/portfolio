What to watch:
- NVDA — watch for any direct company statement on shipment pauses or partner compliance; confirmed duration or wider geographic restrictions would be bearish for near-term AI server demand.
- TSM/ASML and chip supply chains — watch whether tariff proposals or export-control disputes become formal policy; enacted measures would be bearish for cross-border semiconductor volumes, while dilution of proposals would ease risk.
- Oil-sensitive markets and airlines — watch for confirmation of actual Strait of Hormuz supply disruption; sustained disruption would be bullish for crude but bearish for fuel-intensive sectors.
- Norse Atlantic — watch for emergency financing or a bankruptcy filing; funding would reduce immediate collapse risk, while filing would confirm deeper pressure across weaker carriers.
