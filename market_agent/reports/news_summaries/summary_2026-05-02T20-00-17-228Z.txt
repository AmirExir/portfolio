- **Markets pause near records:** The **S&P 500 and Nasdaq hit fresh highs**, powered by tech, while the **Dow was roughly flat** as investors balanced strong earnings against geopolitical and tariff risks.

- **Trade tensions resurfaced:** Markets softened after **Trump threatened higher tariffs on European vehicles**, a reminder that trade policy remains a live risk for autos, industrials, and broader sentiment.

- **Apple helped anchor the tech rally:** **Apple’s strong outlook** suggested it is navigating supply constraints better than feared, reinforcing big-tech leadership even as chip and hardware cost pressures persist.

- **April was a breakout month for stocks:** U.S. equities posted their **best month since the pandemic rebound**, underscoring how quickly risk appetite has returned despite macro and geopolitical uncertainty.

- **Oil shock is rippling through consumers and travel:** The Iran-war-driven fuel surge is hitting hard—**Michigan gas prices neared record highs** and **Spirit Airlines may need government support**, highlighting how energy inflation is spreading into household budgets and weaker carriers.

- **Fed messaging got more complicated:** **Rare dissent at the Fed** showed officials are divided over signaling that the next rate move would be a cut—suggesting policy easing is **less straightforward than markets may hope**.

- **AI is moving deeper into defense:** The **Pentagon struck classified-network deals with major AI firms** including Microsoft, Amazon, and Google, signaling growing government demand for enterprise AI and a bigger strategic role for the sector.

- **Meta faces rising regulatory pressure:** Meta said it could **pull Facebook and Instagram from New Mexico** rather than comply with child-safety requirements, showing how platform regulation is becoming a more direct operational and legal risk.

- **Biotech volatility remains brutal:** A setback for a company seen as a major **future rival to Merck in cancer treatment** triggered a sharp stock drop, a reminder that single trial outcomes can quickly reset valuations.

- **Currency markets remain on intervention watch:** After **Japan’s $34.5 billion intervention**, traders are bracing for more moves to support the yen—important for global FX volatility and multinational earnings.
