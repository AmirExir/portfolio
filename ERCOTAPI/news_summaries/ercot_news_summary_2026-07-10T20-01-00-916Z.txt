- ERCOT posted a ROS-approved Dynamics Working Group Procedure Manual Revision 25 tied to MOD-026-2, indicating an update to the process for generator/model verification and dynamic data handling used in system studies. Even without full file review, this points to revised expectations for how dynamic models are submitted, validated, or maintained. (DWG)
- Operationally, changes to the DWG manual can affect the quality of ERCOT transient-stability and dynamic simulations, which feed reliability assessments, generator interconnection studies, and planning for inverter-based resources and other new generation. Better model governance generally improves confidence in study results used for outage coordination and system performance analysis. (DWG)
- From a planning and governance angle, ROS approval means the revision has moved beyond a working-group draft and may influence ERCOT compliance workflows and interconnection requirements, especially where MOD-026-2 generator model validation supports large-scale resource additions and grid reliability oversight. (DWG)
