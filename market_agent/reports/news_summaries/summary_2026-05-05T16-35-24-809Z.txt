- **Markets are wobbling, but not breaking:** Dow futures fell as oil spiked, though the broader market reaction appears measured after a strong run in equities. Investors seem to be pricing in geopolitical risk without fully abandoning risk assets.

- **Air travel faces a double hit:** Airlines are reportedly cutting flights amid fuel shortage fears tied to the Middle East crisis, reinforcing pressure on an industry already vulnerable to fuel volatility and operational disruptions.

- **Ryan Cohen makes a bold play for eBay:** GameStop’s chairman is pursuing a roughly **$56 billion** buyout of eBay, sending eBay shares sharply higher. Strategically, it’s a high-stakes attempt to turn meme-stock capital into a broader e-commerce challenge to Amazon.

- **Dealmaking remains active despite macro noise:**  
  - American Express Global Business Travel agreed to a **$6.3 billion** take-private deal.  
  - UCB is buying Candid Therapeutics for **$2 billion upfront**.  
  This suggests M&A appetite remains healthy, especially where buyers see strategic assets or discounted opportunities.

- **Enterprise AI consolidation continues:** SAP is acquiring Dremio to better unify SAP and non-SAP data for AI use cases. The bigger theme: companies are racing to own the data stack that powers enterprise AI, not just the models.

- **Anthropic may be moving deeper into Wall Street:** A reported **$1.5 billion joint venture** with major financial firms would show how frontier AI players are increasingly pairing with institutional capital to fund commercialization and infrastructure.

- **Bottom line:** Today’s business story is a split screen — **geopolitical stress is lifting oil and pressuring transport**, while **AI and M&A continue to attract capital**, showing that corporate dealmaking hasn’t frozen even as macro risks intensify.
