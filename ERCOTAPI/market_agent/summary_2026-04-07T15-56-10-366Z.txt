- Multiple articles point to accelerating AI and hyperscale data-center buildout, with vendors, miners, and network suppliers all signaling strong demand growth; for ERCOT, the main takeaway is continued pressure from large-load interconnection requests rather than a single Texas-specific project announcement. This reinforces expectations that Texas will remain a prime target for new data-center campuses given its scale, land availability, and existing power-market structure. (The Verge, PR Newswire UK, Business Insider, GlobeNewswire)

- Several reports highlight a broader industry shift toward pairing AI data centers with dedicated natural-gas generation when grid delivery and capacity additions cannot keep pace. For ERCOT, that raises implications for behind-the-meter generation, gas-electric coordination, emissions tradeoffs, and potential changes in net load shape if hyperscalers seek firm on-site supply instead of relying solely on grid service. (TechCrunch, Wired)

- The emerging pattern of crypto miners pivoting to AI and reusing power-dense sites is particularly relevant to ERCOT because it could convert previously flexible or curtailment-capable mining load into more persistent, higher-value demand. That would affect planning assumptions for reliability, transmission upgrades, and interconnection studies, especially if former mining campuses in Texas seek expansion or reduced load flexibility under AI workloads. (Business Insider, GlobeNewswire, Cointelegraph)
