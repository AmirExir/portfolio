- Geopolitics is driving markets: oil prices surged and U.S. stock futures fell as Trump’s Strait of Hormuz deadline for Iran nears, reinforcing inflation worries and adding pressure on central banks to stay hawkish.

- AI remains the clearest earnings and capex theme: Samsung jumped on a forecast of record profit driven by AI memory-chip demand, while Anthropic reportedly locked in massive chip supply deals with Google and Broadcom to secure future compute.

- Dealmaking and policy are reshaping sectors: Bill Ackman’s Pershing Square is pursuing a roughly €55.8 billion takeover of Universal Music, while U.S. health insurers rallied after stronger-than-expected Medicare Advantage payment rates.
