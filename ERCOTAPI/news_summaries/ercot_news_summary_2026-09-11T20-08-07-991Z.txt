- ERCOT posted NPRR1347 to clarify that Energy Offer Curves for procured Contract for Capacity resources are set at the effective Value of Lost Load, tightening scarcity-price treatment for capacity held under the new dispatch construct and sharpening incentives for performance during stressed conditions. (NPRR1347/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted two fuel-cost and scarcity pricing changes that both matter in tight conditions: NPRR1346 would double the transportation adder in the Fuel Oil Price, while NPRR1345 would extend the Exceptional Fuel Cost process sunset from 2027 to 2030, preserving cost recovery pathways for oil-backed and fuel-stressed generation. (NPRR1346, NPRR1345/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1343 to add a fee for inefficient hourly PTP Obligation bids in the Day-Ahead Market, a market-efficiency measure that could reduce uneconomic congestion trading behavior and improve DAM price formation. (NPRR1343/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1344 to move the Texas Market Test Plan from an Other Binding Document into Protocol Section 19, standardizing approval and governance of market testing and potentially reducing ambiguity around implementation readiness for future market changes. (NPRR1344/ERCOT)
- ERCOT advanced a broad large-load reliability package: NOGRR282 would establish frequency and voltage ride-through requirements for Large Computational Loads, NOGRR289 would allow certain cooling load components up to 365 days after in-service to comply, and PGRR144 would clarify dynamic model submission requirements for large loads so ERCOT can study them more accurately. (NOGRR282, NOGRR289, PGRR144/ERCOT)
- The large-load filings point to a clear policy direction: what happened is ERCOT posted new LCL ride-through and modeling requirements; the operational impact is stronger grid performance during disturbances and better visibility into large-load behavior; the planning/governance implication is stricter interconnection expectations for data centers and other high-impact loads seeking service in ERCOT. (NOGRR282, NOGRR289, PGRR144/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR288, related to NPRR1340, to add Energy Storage Resource participation to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service, a notable reliability-market expansion that could increase fast flexible reserve capability and improve battery monetization in ERCOT ancillary services. (NOGRR288/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR287 on load shed provisions to identify and minimize overlap of essential loads, a blackstart/emergency-operations relevant governance change aimed at making firm load shed more targeted and reducing unintended impacts during rotating outages or emergency curtailments. (NOGRR287/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR286 establishing 765-kV operational voltage limits, signaling continued preparation for extra-high-voltage system development and the operating framework needed for future backbone transmission expansion. (NOGRR286/ERCOT)
- ERCOT planning and interconnection transparency filings also moved forward: PGRR131 would require interconnection cost reporting for transmission-connected generators, PGRR132 clarifies new resources must interconnect via a new SGIA, and PGRR146/PGRR147 adjust planning assessment cadence and contingency load-loss alignment—together improving generator-entry transparency, study consistency, and reliability planning discipline. (PGRR131, PGRR132, PGRR146, PGRR147/ERCOT)
- ERCOT posted OBDRR055 revising the Non-Spinning Reserve deployment and recall procedure for RTC+B alignment, an operational detail with real-time implications because reserve deployment logic and recall timing directly affect operator actions, ancillary service performance, and scarcity management. (OBDRR055/ERCOT)
