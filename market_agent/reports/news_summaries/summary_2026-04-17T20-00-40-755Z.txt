- **Stocks stay resilient:** The **S&P 500 and Nasdaq hit fresh record highs**, suggesting investors are largely looking past Middle East tensions and leaning into continued growth momentum.

- **AI chip demand remains a core market driver:** **ASML, TSMC, and AMD** all benefited from renewed optimism around AI infrastructure spending, reinforcing the view that the semiconductor upcycle is still intact.

- **AMD surged on Wall Street support:** AMD shares jumped sharply after a favorable analyst reaction, a sign investors may be reassessing laggards in the AI chip race.

- **Energy risk is becoming an aviation story:** The IEA warned **Europe may have only about six weeks of jet fuel left** if supply disruptions persist, raising the risk of flight cuts and broader travel-sector pressure.

- **Airlines face a squeeze from both sides:** With fuel costs elevated, **Lufthansa is cutting capacity**, **Spirit reportedly faces possible shutdown/liquidation**, and U.S. airlines are under political pressure to lower fares if fuel prices ease.

- **Consumer staples show pricing limits:** **PepsiCo beat expectations**, helped by price cuts that brought shoppers back—an important signal that volume growth may now require more promotional activity.

- **Retail disruption keeps reshaping legacy models:** **QVC/HSN’s owner filed for Chapter 11**, underscoring how older TV-commerce businesses are struggling against e-commerce and social platforms.

- **Healthcare names moved on outlook and policy:** **Abbott fell** after weak guidance, while **Hims & Hers rose** on hopes the FDA could loosen peptide restrictions—showing how regulation and forward guidance are dominating healthcare sentiment.

- **Labor market remains steady:** **Weekly U.S. jobless claims fell**, pointing to a still-stable employment backdrop despite broader geopolitical and cost concerns.

- **Netflix is a key watch after the bell:** Its earnings are likely to shape sentiment across media and streaming, especially as investors look for clarity on growth strategy and margin durability.
