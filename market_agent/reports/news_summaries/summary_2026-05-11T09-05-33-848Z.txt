- **Rates and debt are back in focus:** The U.S. Treasury now expects to borrow more this quarter as cash flow weakens, reinforcing pressure on long-term yields. Takeaway: higher-for-longer borrowing costs remain a headwind for stocks and the economy.

- **Powell signals continuity at the Fed:** Jerome Powell plans to stay on the Fed’s Board even after his chair term ends, breaking with decades of precedent. Markets may read this as policy continuity, but it could also prolong political tension around rate strategy.

- **Energy shock is widening:** Multiple reports point to the Iran war tightening oil supply, draining inventories, and lifting fuel-related costs globally. Business implication: renewed inflation risk for transport, agriculture, manufacturing, and consumers.

- **Food inflation risk is rising globally:** Higher fuel and fertilizer costs are forcing some Asian farmers to skip planting, threatening crop output. That raises the odds of broader food-price pressure and supply-chain strain.

- **Intel gets a lift from Apple manufacturing talks:** Intel shares jumped on reports of a preliminary chipmaking deal with Apple. If it holds, it would be a major credibility boost for Intel’s foundry ambitions and U.S. semiconductor policy.

- **AI adoption is moving deeper into infrastructure:** The FAA is exploring AI for airspace-routing management, though not for safety-critical functions. This shows government demand for practical AI tools is growing, especially in operational efficiency.

- **Data centers face resource scrutiny:** A Georgia data center reportedly used 30 million gallons of water before the issue was fully noticed. As AI and cloud expansion continue, water and power usage are becoming material regulatory and community risks.

- **Crypto exposure hit Trump Media hard:** Trump Media posted a much wider quarterly loss, driven largely by markdowns on bitcoin and CRO holdings. It’s another reminder that corporate crypto strategies can sharply amplify earnings volatility.

- **Palantir’s AI tension is notable:** Despite benefiting from AI enthusiasm, the company faces concern that general-purpose AI models could erode some of its edge. Investors are increasingly separating “AI narrative” from durable competitive advantage.

- **Consumer strain remains uneven:** Gas-price burden data and auto-lending commentary suggest affordability pressure persists, even if lenders say repayment trends remain stable. Bottom line: consumers are holding up, but the margin for error is shrinking.

- **Food safety alert could affect deli meat sellers:** USDA issued a public health alert for certain headcheese products over possible listeria contamination. Limited market impact, but another signal that food producers and retailers remain exposed to recall and trust risks.
