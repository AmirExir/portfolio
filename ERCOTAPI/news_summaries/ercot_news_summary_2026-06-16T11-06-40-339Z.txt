- ERCOT posted its May 2026 Monthly Operational Overview showing a preliminary monthly peak of 77,822 MW, 577 MW below May 2025’s peak, indicating early-summer demand remained high but did not surpass last year’s May stress level. (BOARD OF DIRECTORS / ERCOT Monthly Operational Overview May 2026)
- The report notes one Grid Watch tied to transmission storm damage in the Rio Grande Valley from May 9–10, highlighting that localized transmission outages—not just systemwide supply tightness—can still drive operational alerts and reliability risk in constrained areas. (BOARD OF DIRECTORS / ERCOT Monthly Operational Overview May 2026)
- May energy generation rose 1.6% year over year to 41,870 GWh, suggesting overall system output kept pace with seasonal load needs; for planners and market participants, the combination of strong energy production, a sub-2025 May peak, and transmission-related watch activity reinforces the importance of both resource adequacy and targeted grid hardening in weather-exposed regions.
