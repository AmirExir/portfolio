- ERCOT posted NPRR1347 to clarify that Energy Offer Curves for procured Contract for Capacity resources will be set at the effective Value of Lost Load; this tightens scarcity-price alignment for any future C4C dispatch, likely strengthening performance incentives during tight conditions and signaling how ERCOT intends to operationalize capacity procurement in market software and settlement logic. (ERCOT NPRR1347)
- ERCOT posted a cluster of fuel and scarcity pricing changes: NPRR1346 would double the transportation-cost adder in the Fuel Oil Price from $0.05/gal to $0.10/gal, while NPRR1345 would extend the exceptional fuel cost process sunset from April 1, 2027 to April 1, 2030; together these changes point to continued accommodation of higher emergency fuel logistics and cost recovery for thermal reliability. (ERCOT NPRR1346; ERCOT NPRR1345)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1343 to introduce a bid fee for inefficient hourly Point-to-Point Obligation bids in the Day-Ahead Market, a targeted market-design step that could reduce uneconomic congestion trading behavior, improve DAM efficiency, and sharpen congestion price signals used for hedging and transmission planning. (ERCOT NPRR1343)
- ERCOT posted NPRR1344 to move the Texas Market Test Plan from an Other Binding Document into Protocol Section 19, which is mainly a governance and change-control move but materially increases standardization and formal oversight of market testing before rule and system changes go live. (ERCOT NPRR1344)
- ERCOT posted several large-load planning and operating changes: PGRR145 creates a transitional “Batch Zero” to study mature large-load interconnection requests on a system-wide basis, PGRR144 clarifies dynamic model submission requirements for large loads including large computational loads, and NOGRR289 would allow certain large computational loads up to 365 days after in-service to meet cooling-load ride-through requirements. Net effect: ERCOT is building a more structured framework for data-center-scale load screening, modeling, and staged compliance. (ERCOT PGRR145; ERCOT PGRR144; ERCOT NOGRR289)
- The large-load package has three clear angles: ERCOT is formalizing how new high-density loads enter the queue, improving dynamic modeling so planners can study voltage/frequency behavior more accurately, and allowing limited post-energization compliance flexibility where technology constraints exist; this supports interconnection throughput but also shows ERCOT is worried about operational performance from rapidly growing large-load additions. (ERCOT PGRR145; ERCOT PGRR144; ERCOT NOGRR289)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR288 related to Dispatchable Reliability Reserve Service participation by Energy Storage Resources, expanding ESR integration into reliability services; this should improve fast-response reserve capability, deepen battery monetization pathways, and further embed storage in ERCOT’s reliability stack rather than only energy arbitrage. (ERCOT NOGRR288)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR287 to improve load-shed coordination between Transmission Operators and TDSPs to identify and minimize overlap of essential loads, a reliability-focused operating change aimed at making involuntary curtailment more precise and reducing the risk that critical facilities are shed unnecessarily during emergency operations. (ERCOT NOGRR287)
- ERCOT posted NOGRR286 to add 765-kV operational voltage limits, a notable transmission-operability milestone that indicates ERCOT is preparing operating rules for extra-high-voltage infrastructure; this has implications for future bulk transfer capability, system strength, and how operators manage voltage on an expanding backbone network. (ERCOT NOGRR286)
- ERCOT posted PGRR147 and PGRR146 to align allowable non-consequential load loss treatment with NERC expectations and to shift large generator/large load stability assessments from quarterly to every four months; these are planning-process changes, but they affect study assumptions, compliance expectations, and the cadence of how ERCOT evaluates grid impacts from new supply and demand additions. (ERCOT PGRR147; ERCOT PGRR146)
- ERCOT posted OBDRR055 updating the Non-Spinning Reserve deployment and recall procedure for RTC+B alignment, suggesting continued refinement of real-time reserve deployment logic as ERCOT integrates newer control-room tools and operating practices into formal procedures. (ERCOT OBDRR055)
