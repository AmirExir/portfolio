- Texas heat, drought, and flash-flood volatility remain an operational risk theme; for ERCOT, more extreme weather raises the chance of sharper summer peaks, localized outages, thermal derates, and tighter reserve conditions, reinforcing the value of weather-sensitive planning and seasonal reliability preparations. (PRNewswire)
- Virtual power plant adoption in Texas is gaining broader attention as households export distributed energy back to the grid; while still small versus ERCOT system load, the trend supports longer-term distribution-grid flexibility, peak shaving, and retail/customer-side participation in reliability support. (NBC News)
- Texas’ natural gas pipeline buildout remains a strategic enabler for ERCOT reliability because it supports fuel deliverability for thermal generation needed to balance renewables and serve large new loads; the planning implication is that gas infrastructure and electric growth are becoming more tightly linked. (The Daily Caller)
