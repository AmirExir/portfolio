- AI/data-center load growth remains the dominant grid theme: multiple reports point to hyperscale and crypto demand reshaping utility investment, siting, and regulation, with likely ERCOT impacts including faster large-load interconnection pressure, higher transmission build needs, and stronger interest in co-located or “bring your own power” supply models. (Forbes; Fortune)

- A key ERCOT planning tension is emerging around who pays for rapid load growth: one study warns data centers and crypto mining could materially raise power costs, while an industry-backed report disputes direct residential bill impacts; for ERCOT, that debate matters for cost allocation, tariff design, load flexibility requirements, and political support for infrastructure expansion. (Futurity: Research News; PRNewswire)

- Local backlash to Texas data-center development is intensifying, with reports of a one-year rural data-center moratorium in Hill County and growing community opposition elsewhere; even outside ERCOT rulemaking, this can slow load additions, shift project geography, complicate transmission planning assumptions, and increase pressure for clearer state/local governance over large-load siting. (Tom’s Hardware UK; Business Insider)

- Texas is increasingly being marketed as a destination for dedicated power-backed AI infrastructure, including NRG’s “bring your own power” strategy and reporting on tax incentives for a Chevron-linked data-center power plant; for ERCOT, that signals more behind-the-meter or directly supplied large-load proposals that could reduce some system stress but raise market, reliability, and cost-shift questions. (Fortune; Grist)

- Broader utility consolidation and infrastructure positioning around AI demand—highlighted by NextEra’s Dominion deal—reinforces the competitive pressure on ERCOT to remain the fastest market for new generation, transmission, and large-load connections; Texas could benefit from that demand pull, but only if interconnection, deliverability, and reliability processes keep pace. (Fortune; Reuters/The Times of India)

- Crypto-linked load remains relevant to ERCOT’s demand outlook: Canaan reported expansion across joint-mining projects, while Soluna said data-center hosting now exceeds mining revenue, underscoring how flexible mining sites may evolve into AI or mixed digital-load campuses. For ERCOT, that means some “crypto” load may become more persistent, less interruptible, and more transmission-intensive over time. (PRNewswire; Cointelegraph)
